    Discover trends around the world

    Is it a real trend, a natural cycle, or just noise? Ask NASA data in three steps.

    • The problemA natural cycle or one dataset can look like a trend. In our global rain scan, only 1 in 4 "trends" was confirmed by a second NASA dataset.
    • Our solutionFour tests and a second witness, for any place on Earth, in seconds. One clear answer.
    • The impactYou see if the trend is a risk and what to do. Fewer false alarms, faster action on real change.

    How TrendSleuth works

    1. 1Remove the seasonsEach month is compared with its own normal value.
    2. 2Remove natural rhythmsWe test 11 phenomena, like El Niño, and take out the one that matters.
    3. 3Test the trendFour statistical tests must agree before we call it a trend.
    4. 4Ask a second witnessFor rain, a second NASA dataset must agree. If not, it is a false lead.
    Reading the globeRed: confirmed fall. Green: confirmed rise. Stronger colour means faster change. No colour means no confirmed trend: it may be noise or a natural cycle.

    Warning. A confirmed trend is continued in a straight line to 2050 and compared with the normal ups and downs of the place. It is a sign to investigate, not a climate-model forecast. Advice: IPCC AR6 adaptation options.
